## Out of scope this phase (explicit, not omissions)

Per the brief's own "out of scope" list, none of the following were implemented, and nothing above should be read as claiming they were: automatic Bud cohort creation/transfers/allocation changes, an employer portal, learner self-service, SMS notifications, predictive analytics, a shutdown-week calendar, Power BI deployment, historical attendance reconstruction/transfer from Bud, or any unrelated feature change.
